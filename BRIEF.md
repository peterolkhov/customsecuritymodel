# Event brief — Own Your Intelligence Hackathon (YC, SF, 2026-09-27)

## Goal (verbatim)

Extend QM and GBrain. Push further with Memorable, Superset and UFO. Ship something that didn't exist this morning. This isn't a pitch competition.

## Rules

- Build something using GBrain (required)
- No prebuilt projects / forks of existing projects
- Must build during hackathon hours
